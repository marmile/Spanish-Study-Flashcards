## Learning tip

Learn nouns together with their article: **la casa**, not just *casa*;
**el libro**, not just *libro*.

For irregular verbs, learn the infinitive together with the first-person
form, for example:

-   **tener → tengo**
-   **hacer → hago**
-   **ir → voy**
-   **poner → pongo**
-   **salir → salgo**
