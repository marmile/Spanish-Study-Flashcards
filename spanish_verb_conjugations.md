# Spanish Verb Conjugations

## Subject pronouns

  Español                   English
  ------------------------- ------------------------------
  yo                        I
  tú                        you (informal singular)
  él / ella / usted         he / she / you (formal)
  nosotros/as               we
  vosotros/as               you (informal plural, Spain)
  ellos / ellas / ustedes   they / you (plural)

> **Note:** This file uses the **present indicative (presente de
> indicativo)**, the main present tense used at A1 level.

## Conjugation patterns

Regular **-ar** verbs: `-o, -as, -a, -amos, -áis, -an`\
Regular **-er** verbs: `-o, -es, -e, -emos, -éis, -en`\
Regular **-ir** verbs: `-o, -es, -e, -imos, -ís, -en`

## Quick examples

-   **Yo hablo español.** --- I speak Spanish.
-   **Tú tienes un libro.** --- You have a book.
-   **Él vive en España.** --- He lives in Spain.
-   **Ella trabaja en un hospital.** --- She works in a hospital.
-   **Usted habla español.** --- You speak Spanish. (formal)
-   **Nosotros comemos en casa.** --- We eat at home.
-   **Vosotros estudiáis español.** --- You all study Spanish. (Spain)
-   **Ellos van al trabajo.** --- They go to work.
-   **Ustedes quieren café.** --- You all want coffee.

## Important point

Spanish often omits the subject pronoun because the verb ending already
tells you who is doing the action. For example, **hablo español**
normally means **I speak Spanish** without needing to say **yo**.
